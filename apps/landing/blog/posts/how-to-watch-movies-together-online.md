Watching movies with friends online sounds like it should be one button. In practice there are three ways people do it, and they feel very different once the film is more than ten minutes in. Here is what each one is like, and how to set up the one that holds up for a whole evening.

## The three ways to watch a movie together online

### 1. Count down and press play at the same time

Everyone opens the film, someone types "3, 2, 1, play" in the group chat, and you all hit the button together. It costs nothing and needs nothing installed.

It also drifts. One person's player buffers for a second, another pauses to answer the door, and twenty minutes later someone laughs at a joke the rest of you haven't heard yet. Getting back in step means another countdown, and nobody knows who is ahead. We wrote up [why players drift apart](why-watch-party-out-of-sync.html) if you want the details.

### 2. Share your screen on a call

One person plays the film and shares their screen on Zoom, Google Meet or Discord. Everyone else watches a live copy of that screen.

It works for a short clip. For a full movie the call squeezes the picture to fit everyone's connection, so dark scenes turn muddy and fast scenes break into blocks. The host's upload speed sets the quality for the whole group, and only the host can pause. On Netflix and other copy-protected services, the shared window often arrives as a black rectangle. Our post on [streaming Netflix on Discord](screen-share-movie-discord-zoom.html) explains why.

### 3. Use a watch party extension

Everyone opens the film on their own computer and account, and an extension keeps those players on the same second. When anyone pauses, every player pauses. When someone skips back to catch a line, the whole room goes back with them.

Nobody's screen is shared, so everyone gets the full picture their own connection can handle. The only thing passing between you is the playback state: playing or paused, and at which second.

### Which one to use

| | Countdown | Screen share | Sync extension |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Picture quality | Full | Compressed by the call | Full |
| Stays in sync | No, drifts | Yes, one stream | Yes |
| Who can pause | Everyone, separately | Only the host | Anyone, for everyone |
| Works on Netflix | Yes | Often a black screen | Yes |
| Everyone needs access to the film | Yes | No | Yes |
| Setup | None | A call app | A browser extension |

For anything longer than a clip, the extension wins. The one case where screen sharing is the better pick is a video only you have, like a home video or a file on your laptop, because nobody else can open it themselves.

## What everyone needs

- **A computer with Chrome.** Watchbear is a Chrome extension, so phones and tablets can't join.
- **A Google account.** Everyone signs in with Google. There is no separate account or password.
- **Their own access to the film.** If it's on Netflix, everyone needs a Netflix account that can play it. If it's a free video on YouTube or Vimeo, nobody needs to pay for anything.
- **The same title in their country.** Streaming catalogs differ by country, so a film one friend can see may be missing for another. Ask everyone to search for it before the night.

## How to watch a movie together with Watchbear

### 1. Add the extension

Install Watchbear from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/watchbear-watch-together/ldegfikaldilbcpgmiopdnnhpkpcnepn), pin the bear to your toolbar, and sign in with Google.

### 2. Open the film

Go to the site the movie is on and start it so the player loads. Watchbear finds the main video on the page, so it works on YouTube, Vimeo, Netflix and most sites with a standard video player. Netflix gets dedicated handling for its player.

### 3. Start a party

Click the bear and choose **Start a party with this video**. A room opens in Chrome's side panel, next to the film rather than over it.

### 4. Send the invite link

Copy the invite link from the panel and drop it in your group chat, or read out the short room code. A friend who opens the link without Watchbear sees an install button first, then lands on the same film inside the room.

### 5. Press play once everyone is in

Anyone in the room can play, pause or skip, and every player follows.

> **Tip:** start the party ten minutes early. Sign-ins, profile pickers and a missing extension are the usual reasons a movie night starts late, and they are easier to sort out before the opening credits.

## Watching a movie together with a video call

Text chat is fine for a comedy. For a thriller or a finale you may want to see faces. Watchbear has voice and video chat built in, free for everyone, so you don't need a separate call running in another window.

- **Cameras start off.** Every time you join a room your camera is off, and turning it on is one click.
- **Up to 8 people on camera.** A room holds up to 50 people, and 8 of them can have camera and microphone on at once.
- **The film comes first.** Calls go directly between the people in the room, and if a computer struggles, Watchbear lowers the camera quality before the film stutters.

Wear headphones when you're on voice. Otherwise your microphone picks up the film's sound and sends it back to everyone a moment later. More on this in our post about [watch parties with video chat](watch-party-with-video-chat.html).

## If you're watching from another country

Two things change when your friends are abroad. Catalogs differ, so a film on your Netflix may not exist on theirs. Pick something you have both found before the night, or pick a free video that is open everywhere. Time zones also matter: agree on the start time in both local times, and write both down in the chat.

Couples and friends who do this every week can plan it in the extension. Our guide to [watching movies together long distance](how-to-watch-movies-together-long-distance.html) covers reminders, time zones and picking up a series where you stopped.

## When something goes wrong

### Someone is a few seconds off

Skip back a few seconds. The skip moves every player in the room, so everyone lands on the same frame again. If one person keeps drifting, have them reload the page and open the invite link again.

### Watchbear doesn't find the video

Play the video once so the player loads, then open the panel again. Some sites load the player only after you press play.

### The film is missing for one person

That is the regional catalog or a subscription that doesn't include it. Nothing in the room can fix it, so switch to a title everyone can open.
