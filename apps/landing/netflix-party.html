Netflix watch party in three steps

Everyone needs Chrome on a laptop or desktop, a Google account to sign in, and their own Netflix account. Netflix catalogs differ by country, so check the title is there for everyone first.

  1. Add Watchbear and start a party

    Install Watchbear from the Chrome Web Store, pin the bear to your toolbar and sign in with Google. Open the film or episode on Netflix, click the bear, and choose Start a party with this video.

    The room opens in Chrome's side panel next to the player, so nothing covers the picture.

  2. Send the invite link

    Copy the invite link from the panel and send it in your group chat, or read out the short room code. A friend who opens the link without Watchbear sees an install button first, then lands on the same title.

    Each friend signs in to their own Netflix. Only the position in the film travels between you.

  3. Press play and talk

    Anyone in the room can play, pause or skip, and every player follows. Turn on your microphone or camera from the panel to talk over the film. Cameras start off each time you join.

    Chat and emoji reactions sit in the same panel, beside the video.

Netflix party questions

Is Netflix Party still available?

Not under that name. The extension that was called Netflix Party is now Teleparty, and its website describes it as Teleparty (formerly Netflix Party). Watchbear is a separate extension that does the same core job: it keeps everyone's Netflix player on the same second.

Can I join a Netflix party from my phone?

Not with Watchbear. It runs in Chrome on a laptop or desktop computer only. Teleparty has iOS and Android apps that support Netflix, Disney+, Hulu and HBO Max, according to its support page.

Does everyone need their own Netflix account?

Yes. Watchbear only shares the playback position and play or pause, never the video. Each person streams the title from a Netflix account they can use, so everyone needs access to it.

Is a Netflix watch party with Watchbear free?

Yes. Watchbear is currently free to use, including voice and video chat, and nothing asks for a card. Everyone signs in with a Google account. The Netflix subscription is the only cost, and each person pays for their own.

Does it work on Hulu, Prime Video or Disney+?

Netflix is the only subscription service with dedicated handling in Watchbear. On Hulu, Prime Video or Disney+, Watchbear looks for the main video on the page as it does on any site, and copy-protected players behave differently from site to site. Try it with one friend before a big night.

How many people can join?

Up to 50 people in a Watchbear room, and up to 8 of them on camera and microphone at once.
