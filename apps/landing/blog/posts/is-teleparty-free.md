Short answer: yes, Teleparty is free to use, with limits. The free plan syncs video and has text chat on the main streaming services. Teleparty Premium is a paid subscription that adds more services, voice and video chat, and a few extras.

Everything about Teleparty below comes from Teleparty's [support page](https://www.teleparty.com/support), checked November 28, 2026. Prices and plans can change, so check that page before you pay.

> Watchbear is a different watch party extension and isn't affiliated with Teleparty. We compare the two at the end.

## What the free plan includes

Teleparty's support page describes the free version as "basic video synchronization and text chat," and you can start parties on the free supported services:

- Netflix
- Disney+
- Hulu
- Amazon
- YouTube
- HBO Max

The browser extension runs on Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari and Opera. Firefox is not supported. There are also iOS and Android apps, which support fewer services than the browser extension.

The party size limit is the same for everyone: Teleparty says up to 1,000 people can join one party.

## What Teleparty Premium adds

### More streaming services

Premium lets you host parties on Crunchyroll, Paramount+ and ESPN+, in addition to the free services. In Teleparty's words, Premium allows "unlimited watch parties on ALL of our supported streaming services."

### Voice and video chat

This is the one most people notice. Only Premium users can turn on their camera and microphone. Everyone in the party can see and hear the Premium users, whatever their own plan.

So in a group of four where one person pays, that person can talk and be seen, and the other three can only type. For everyone to talk, everyone needs Premium.

### Extras

Premium members also get premium badges and can customize their chat. Teleparty adds that Premium subscribers directly support its development.

## How much Teleparty Premium costs

| Plan | Price | Free trial |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | $6.59 a month | No |
| Annual | $3.99 a month, billed as $47.88 a year | 7 days |

You need a Google account to sign in and upgrade. The upgrade is in the extension: install it, sign in with Google, click Upgrade and enter your payment details.

## Who in a party needs to pay

This is where people get confused, so here it is plainly, from Teleparty's support page:

- **To start a party on Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Amazon, YouTube or HBO Max:** nobody needs Premium.
- **To start a party on Crunchyroll, Paramount+ or ESPN+:** the host needs Premium. Friends can join with the free version.
- **To talk or be on camera:** each person who wants to turn on their own camera or microphone needs Premium.

Everyone still needs their own account on the streaming service itself. Teleparty doesn't share anyone's subscription; it only keeps the players in sync.

## How to cancel Teleparty Premium

You can cancel at any time:

1. Click the TP button and sign in.
2. Open the gear icon and choose to manage your subscription.
3. Go to the Billing tab and select **Cancel Subscription**, then confirm.

Premium stays active until the end of your current billing cycle. Teleparty says it does not refund unused time, so cancelling right after a renewal still leaves you paying for that period.

## Is Teleparty Premium worth it?

It depends on what your group watches and how it talks.

- **If you watch on the free services and talk in a separate call,** the free plan covers you.
- **If you watch anime on Crunchyroll or sport on ESPN+,** Premium is the only way to host there with Teleparty.
- **If everyone joins from phones,** Teleparty's apps are the reason to pick it, and Premium decides whether you can talk in them.
- **If you mostly want to see each other while you watch,** count how many people would need Premium. Calls are per person, not per party.
