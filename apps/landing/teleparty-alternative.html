Common questions

Is Watchbear a good Teleparty alternative?

Yes. Watchbear covers the core watch party features people use Teleparty for: synced playback, room codes, and a live chat panel next to the video. On top of that it is open source and can be self-hosted, so you control the server your rooms run on.

Which sites does Watchbear support compared to Teleparty?

Teleparty supports a fixed list of streaming services. Watchbear takes a different approach: it detects the main video on the page, so it works on most sites with a standard video player, such as YouTube and Vimeo. Support on DRM-protected streaming services can vary by site.

Is Watchbear free?

Yes. Watchbear is free and open source, with no paid tier. You sign in with your Google account on the public server, or run your own server without accounts.

How do I switch from Teleparty to Watchbear?

Install the Watchbear Chrome extension, open any video, and start a room. Share the room code or invite link with friends and they join in seconds.
