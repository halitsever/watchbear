A Discord watch party usually starts the same way: someone says "let's watch something" in the server, everyone piles into a voice channel, and then the question is how to get the same video on everyone's screen. Discord has two built-in answers, and a third option sits outside Discord. Each one fits a different kind of night.

## Option 1: the Watch Together activity

Watch Together is one of Discord's Activities, small apps that open inside a voice call. Discord's [Watch Together FAQ](https://support-apps.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/26502500234519-Watch-Together-FAQ) describes it as "a collaborative way to watch YouTube videos with others."

### How to start it

1. Join a voice channel in a server, or start a voice call in a DM or group chat.
2. Press the App Launcher button at the bottom of the call.
3. Pick Watch Together. You'll be asked to authorize it the first time.
4. Friends in the same voice channel join the Activity from its tile.

Discord asks you to turn on Hardware Acceleration in User Settings, Advanced, before starting any Activity on desktop.

### What it does well

Everyone can add YouTube videos to a shared playlist, by search or by pasting a link, and the playlist plays in order. Discord handles the sync, so there is no countdown. Volume is local, so turning your own sound down doesn't affect anyone else.

### The limits

- **YouTube only.** No Netflix, no Disney+, no other sites.
- **Host controls by default.** The person who started it controls play, pause and skipping. The host can switch to Sharing mode so everyone has control. Discord suggests Hosting for big groups and Sharing for small ones.
- **A playback cap.** Discord's FAQ says playback is limited to 180 minutes when watching with a group and 30 minutes alone, followed by a 10-minute cool-down. It also pauses after 30 minutes without interaction.
- **Server admins can turn it off.** The Use Activities permission controls who can launch Activities, so the button may be missing in some servers.
- **English only.** The Activity itself is in English, though you can search for videos in any language.

For a round of music videos or a few YouTube clips, it is the easiest option on this page.

## Option 2: Go Live screen sharing

Go Live streams your screen or one window to the voice channel. Anyone in the channel clicks your stream to watch.

It works with anything you can open, which makes it the right choice for a video only you have, like a recording on your own computer. For films from streaming services it has two problems. The stream is compressed like any call, so a full film looks soft and choppy, and the higher resolutions belong to Discord's paid plan. And Netflix, like many services, protects its video, so viewers often see a black screen with the sound still playing.

We cover both problems in detail in [how to stream Netflix on Discord, and why the screen goes black](screen-share-movie-discord-zoom.html).

## Option 3: Discord for voice, a sync extension for the film

The third way keeps the part of Discord that works, the voice channel, and moves the video out of it. Everyone opens the film in Chrome on their own account, and a watch party extension keeps all the players on the same second. A pause from anyone pauses everyone.

Because nobody streams their screen, everybody gets the full picture, Netflix included, and nobody's upload speed limits the group.

### How to set it up with Watchbear

1. Everyone installs [Watchbear](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/watchbear-watch-together/ldegfikaldilbcpgmiopdnnhpkpcnepn) in Chrome and signs in with Google.
2. Join your Discord voice channel as usual.
3. One person opens the film, clicks the bear and chooses **Start a party with this video**.
4. Copy the invite link from the side panel and paste it into the server's text channel. Anyone who opens it lands on the same film in the room.
5. Press play. Anyone in the room can pause or skip.

> **Wear headphones.** With Discord voice open and the film playing on speakers, your microphone sends the film back into the channel, slightly delayed. Headphones fix it.

You can also drop Discord for the night. Watchbear's side panel has text chat, emoji reactions and voice and video chat for up to 8 people at once, in a room of up to 50.

## Which option to use

| | Watch Together | Go Live | Sync extension |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sites | YouTube only | Anything on your screen | Netflix, YouTube, Vimeo and most standard players |
| Picture | YouTube's own player | Compressed stream | Each person's own player at full quality |
| Netflix | No | Often a black screen | Yes, each on their own account |
| Who controls playback | Host, or everyone in Sharing mode | Only the person streaming | Anyone in the room |
| Time limit | 180 minutes with a group, then a cool-down | None from Discord's side | None |
| Phones | Yes, Discord's FAQ covers mobile | Depends on the Discord app | No, Chrome on a computer |
| Everyone needs access to the video | No, it's YouTube | No | Yes |

Short version: YouTube clips, use Watch Together. A file only you have, use Go Live. A film from a streaming service, use an extension and keep Discord for voice.

## A Netflix watch party on Discord, step by step

Since "netflix watch party discord" is the question most people actually have, here it is in one place.

1. Make sure everyone in the group has a Netflix account that can play the title. Catalogs differ by country.
2. Open a voice channel and tell everyone to open Chrome on a computer.
3. Everyone installs Watchbear. This takes about a minute.
4. The host opens the title on Netflix, starts a party from the bear and posts the invite link in the text channel.
5. Everyone opens the link, signs in to their own Netflix if asked, and lands on the same title.
6. Press play. Talk in Discord, react in the side panel.

If you're watching a series, set **Follow the host's video** to Auto in the side panel, so everyone's tab moves to the next episode when the host's does.
