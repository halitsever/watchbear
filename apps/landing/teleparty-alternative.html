A free Teleparty alternative

Watchbear is a free watch party extension for Chrome. It syncs playback on YouTube, Netflix, Vimeo and most sites with a video player, and voice and video chat cost nothing. Teleparty does some things better, so here is an honest look at both.

Where Teleparty is stronger

A named list of streaming services

Teleparty lists the services it supports, including Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max, Prime Video, Crunchyroll, Paramount+ and ESPN+. Watchbear looks for the main video on any page instead. That reaches more sites, but Netflix is the only big streaming service with dedicated handling, and other DRM-protected services can behave differently from site to site.

Phones

Teleparty has iOS and Android apps for Netflix, Disney+, Hulu and HBO Max. Watchbear runs in Chrome on a laptop or desktop, so everyone in the room needs a computer.

More browsers

The Teleparty extension works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Opera. Watchbear is a Chrome extension.

Very large parties

Teleparty allows up to 1,000 people in one party. A Watchbear room holds up to 50.

Where Watchbear is stronger

Video and voice chat at no cost

In Teleparty, only Premium users can turn on their own camera and microphone. In Watchbear anyone can, up to 8 people at once, and the call runs peer to peer between the people in the room. Your camera starts off every time you join.

Sites that are not on anyone's list

Because Watchbear finds the video on the page, it also works on sites like Vimeo or a course platform, as long as the page uses a standard video player. You don't have to check a list before inviting friends.

Nothing behind a paywall today

Watchbear is currently free to use, and video chat is part of that. Teleparty's free plan covers the main services with text chat, and Premium unlocks calls and the rest of its services.

If your group watches on computers and wants to talk face to face, try Watchbear. If people will join from their phones, or you watch mostly on Disney+, Hulu or HBO Max, Teleparty is the safer choice.

Common questions

Is Watchbear a good Teleparty alternative?

It is if your group watches on computers and wants to see and hear each other without paying. Watchbear syncs YouTube, Netflix, Vimeo and most sites with a standard video player, and its voice and video chat are free. If friends want to join from a phone, or you mostly watch Disney+, Hulu or HBO Max, Teleparty covers that better with its mobile apps and its list of supported services.

Which sites does Watchbear support compared to Teleparty?

Teleparty supports a named list of services. Its free plan covers Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Prime Video, YouTube and HBO Max, and Premium adds the rest, such as Crunchyroll, Paramount+ and ESPN+. Watchbear has no list. It finds the main video on the page, so it works on YouTube, Vimeo and most sites with a standard video player, and it has dedicated handling for Netflix. Support on other DRM-protected streaming services can vary by site.

Does Teleparty have free video chat?

Not for turning on your own camera. Teleparty's support page says voice and video chat are for Premium users, and the rest of the party can see and hear them. In Watchbear anyone in the room can switch on a camera or microphone for free, up to 8 people at once.

Is Watchbear free?

Yes. Watchbear is currently free to use, including voice and video chat. Everyone signs in with their Google account. On paid services like Netflix, each person still needs their own subscription, because everyone plays their own copy.

How do I switch from Teleparty to Watchbear?

Install the Watchbear Chrome extension, open any video, and start a room. Share the room code or invite link with friends and they join in seconds.
