You send the link, everyone clicks it, and nothing happens. Or the TP button is gone, or the party starts for you and not for your friend. Teleparty problems are frustrating mostly because they show up right when everyone is waiting.

The good news is that most of them have a short list of causes. Below are the fixes in the order worth trying, starting with the ones Teleparty itself recommends on its [support page](https://www.teleparty.com/support) (checked November 28, 2026).

> Watchbear is a different watch party extension, not affiliated with Teleparty. We can't fix Teleparty, but everything below about Teleparty comes from its own support page.

## 1. Reinstall to get the latest version

This is Teleparty's first answer for almost every problem: "please uninstall and reinstall Teleparty to make sure you have the latest version." Streaming sites change their players often, and an old copy of the extension can stop recognizing them.

Remove Teleparty from Chrome's extensions page, add it again from the store, and reload the video page. Everyone in the party should do this, not just the host. An old version on one friend's computer is enough to break the party for them.

## 2. Check that you are on a video page

A party only starts from a page where a show is actually playing. Teleparty's support page says to confirm you're logged in to a supported service, on your main profile, with a show playing, and that the address should look like a watch page, such as `netflix.com/watch/` followed by a number.

The Netflix home screen, a title's details page or the "Who's watching?" profile picker won't work. Press play on the episode first, then start the party.

## 3. Find the TP button

If the TP button is missing from your toolbar, Chrome may have tucked it away. Click the puzzle-piece extensions menu, find Teleparty, and pin it. Teleparty suggests checking that hidden menu first and reinstalling if the button is still not there.

## 4. Use a supported browser

Teleparty's browser extension runs on Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari and Opera. Its support page says Firefox and Internet Explorer are not supported. If one friend is on Firefox, that is the problem, and the fix is for them to use one of the other browsers for the night.

## 5. Check the service is on your plan

Teleparty's free plan covers Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Amazon, YouTube and HBO Max. Crunchyroll, Paramount+ and ESPN+ need Teleparty Premium. If a party works on Netflix but not on Crunchyroll, this is the likely reason, and it isn't a bug. [Is Teleparty free?](is-teleparty-free.html) lists what each plan includes.

The same goes for the camera and microphone. Only Premium users can turn them on. Everyone can see and hear the Premium users in the party, but a free user's camera button won't do anything.

## 6. Make sure everyone can open the title

A watch party doesn't send the video to anyone. Each person streams it from their own account. That means two things can quietly break a party:

- **Regional catalogs.** Not every service, or every title, is available in every country. Teleparty's support page tells you to check that the show is available to everyone.
- **Shared accounts.** If two people use the same account, the plan has to allow that many screens at once. Netflix will stop one of them otherwise.

## 7. On Android, reset the Netflix side

For Netflix errors in Teleparty's Android app, its support page suggests restarting the app and selecting the video again, resetting your Netflix password because household restrictions can interfere, and trying a different network or mobile data.

## 8. Rule out other extensions

This one isn't from Teleparty's page, it is general Chrome advice. Extensions that change how pages load, such as ad blockers or script blockers, can get in the way of any extension that reads a video player. Open the video in a window where those are turned off and try again. If the party works, turn them back on one at a time to find the culprit.

## 9. Contact Teleparty

If nothing above helps, Teleparty asks you to email team@teleparty.com. Say which browser and service you're using, what you see, and what you have already tried. It saves a round of questions.

## If you just need tonight's party to work

Sometimes the best fix is a different tool for the evening. [Watchbear](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/watchbear-watch-together/ldegfikaldilbcpgmiopdnnhpkpcnepn) is a free Chrome extension that does the same core job: it keeps everyone's player on the same second while each person streams from their own account.

Where it differs:

- **Video and voice chat are free for everyone,** up to 8 people on camera at once in a room of up to 50.
- **No fixed list of sites.** It finds the main video on the page, so it works on Netflix (with dedicated handling), YouTube, Vimeo and most sites with a standard player. Other subscription services have no dedicated handling, so test one with a friend before a big night.
- **Chrome on a computer only.** There is no phone app and no Firefox, Edge or Safari version. If someone in your group must join from a phone, Teleparty's apps are the better pick.

Teleparty also supports bigger parties, up to 1,000 people. Our [Watchbear vs Teleparty comparison](../teleparty-alternative.html) goes through every difference, including where Teleparty is the stronger choice.
