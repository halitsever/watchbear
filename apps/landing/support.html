Quick fixes

Camera or microphone is blocked
Chrome asks once. If you declined, allow the camera and microphone for the site in Chrome's site settings, then switch them on again in the panel.
Another app is using the camera
Close the other call or app that holds the device, then switch your camera back on.
My camera switched itself off
When a computer is struggling, Watchbear lowers the picture it sends and can turn the camera off so the film keeps playing. Switch it back on whenever you like.
Watchbear says no video was found
Let the video play once on the page, then reopen the Watchbear panel. It looks for the main video player on the page.
A friend is a few seconds behind
Play, pause and skip reach everyone within a moment, and a player that drifts is nudged back to the room's position.
A friend can't join from their phone
Watchbear is a Chrome extension, so everyone needs Chrome on a laptop or desktop and a Google account to sign in.

Getting started

How do I start a watch party?
Add Watchbear to Chrome from the Chrome Web Store, open the video you want to watch, click the bear and start a room. Send the invite link or the short room code to your friends.
How do my friends join?
They install Watchbear in Chrome, sign in with their Google account and open your invite link, or type the room code in the panel. Once they are in, everyone's player follows play, pause and skip.
How many people can join?
Up to 50 people in a room, and up to 8 of them on camera and microphone at once.
Does it cost anything?
No. Hosting, joining, video and voice chat, messages and reactions are all free, and nothing asks for a card.

Sites and subscriptions

Which sites work?
Netflix, YouTube, Vimeo and other pages built on a standard video player. Netflix gets dedicated handling for its player. On other sites Watchbear looks for the main video on the page.
Does everyone need their own Netflix account?
Yes. On paid services each person signs in to their own account. Watchbear shares only the playback position, never the film itself, so everyone plays their own copy at full quality.
Is this screen sharing?
No. Nothing is streamed from one screen to another, so the picture stays sharp and the sound stays in step with it.

Privacy

Do calls go through Watchbear's servers?
No. Voice and video travel peer to peer between the people in the room. The server only introduces participants to each other, and messages are not stored on it.
Is my camera on when I join?
No. The camera starts off every time you arrive, so you are never seen before you choose to switch it on.
